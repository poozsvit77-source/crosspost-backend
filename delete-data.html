According to Facebook Platform terms, you have the right to request the deletion of your data associated with PE5.

How to Remove PE5 from your Facebook Account:

  1. Go to your Facebook Account’s **Settings & Privacy** and click **Settings**.
  2. Scroll down and look for **Apps and Websites**.
  3. Locate **PE5** in the list of active apps.
  4. Click the **Remove** button to disconnect and delete your data from our system.
